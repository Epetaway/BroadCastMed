Early Symptom Presentation in Colorectal Cancer: What Primary Care Providers Need to Know

With rising incidence of colorectal cancer in adults under 50, early recognition of atypical presentations is crucial. This symposium provides practical guidance for primary care and gastroenterology providers.

⚠️ Red Flag Symptoms:

  • Rectal bleeding in patients <45 years
  • Persistent change in bowel habits (>6 weeks)
  • Unexplained iron-deficiency anemia
  • New-onset abdominal pain with weight loss

Learning Objectives:

  • Identify high-risk presentations requiring immediate referral
  • Understand age-appropriate screening modifications
  • Apply clinical decision tools for diagnostic workup
  • Recognize when to expedite colonoscopy referral
